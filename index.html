From request to receipt, one connected trip.

Every ride follows the same five steps, whether it is a quick hop across town or a booking made the day before.

Ask for a ride

Enter where you are going. The fare appears straight away, before you commit to anything.

Nearby drivers are asked

The closest drivers who are online get the job first. If nobody answers, the search widens a little at a time.

A driver accepts

You see your driver and vehicle, and follow them to your pickup.

Start with a code

A one-time code confirms the right passenger is in the right car before the trip begins.

Pay and rate

Pay by card or cash, or have the ride billed to a company account. Then rate your driver from one to five stars.

Built carefully, because people rely on it.

Trips, payments and personal details are handled with security first, not as an afterthought.

Card details stay with the bank

Cards are paid on Windcave's secure payment page. FleetConnect never sees or stores your card number.

Locked-down sign-ins

Staff and company panels ask for an emailed one-time code on every sign-in, and admin access is limited to approved people.

Tired drivers stay off the road

Drivers are logged off automatically after long shifts and must take a proper break before they can work again.
